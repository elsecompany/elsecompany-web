else support

We're here to help.

Contact us

Write to else@elsecompany.com and we'll get back to you within 2 business days. If it's about an order, include the email of your else account.

How do I join else?

else is invitation-only. Ask a member for an invitation code, or join the waitlist at elsecompany.com and we'll email you when there's a spot for you.

My order

You can follow your orders in the app, in Profile, My closet, Bought. If something is wrong with a piece you received, write to us and we'll help, including with a refund where appropriate.

else AI

Ask else, your style and photo suggestions use AI from Anthropic, our AI provider, only if you allow it. You can turn else AI on or off any time in Settings, else AI. Learn more in Settings, How Else AI works.

Reporting and blocking

Use Report on any post, reply, piece or profile that doesn't feel right; our team reviews every report within 24 hours. To block a member, tap … on their post or profile and choose Block. You can unblock them in Settings, Blocked accounts.

Deleting your account

Go to Settings, Delete account. This permanently erases your personal data. See our Privacy Policy for details.
