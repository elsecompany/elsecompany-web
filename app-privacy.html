Privacy Policy for the else app

Effective date: November 22, 2026 · Leer en español

This policy explains what personal information The Else Company ("Else", "we", "us") collects through the else app for iPhone, why, who we share it with and the choices you have. For our website, see the website privacy policy.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The Else Company, 1450 Chestnut Street, San Francisco, CA 94123, United States, is responsible for your data (the "controller"). For any privacy question or request, write to else@elsecompany.com.

2. What we collect

  • Your account: your name, username, email, password (never stored in plain text), the invitation you joined with, your city, the confirmation that you are 18 or older and, if you add it, your birthday and profile photo. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive the identifier and email they share with us.
  • Your style: your sizes, the pieces and brands you like or pass on in the style swipe, the style we work out from them, and the pieces you love.
  • Your activity in Community: the people you follow, your posts, replies, likes and mentions, what you are looking for, and the members you block or report.
  • Your conversation with else (Ask else): the messages you write and else's answers, kept so you can come back to them. You can clear the conversation at any time.
  • Purchases and offers: the pieces you buy and your offers, your shipping address and, if you give it, your phone number. Payments are processed by Stripe and Apple Pay: we never see or store your full card number.
  • Photos of pieces you sell: the photos you upload when you list a piece.
  • Your device: a push notification token, so we can send you notifications if you allow them.

If you tap Use my location when adding an address, your iPhone finds the street, city and ZIP code where you are and sends only that address to us. We do not store your location. Weather on the Home screen is shown for your city.

3. How we use it

  • To run the marketplace: show pieces in your area, process your orders and deliveries, and let you follow, post and reply in Community.
  • To personalize else: pick pieces for your style and size and tailor Ask else.
  • To send you notifications and messages from else about your pieces, offers, orders and the people you follow. You can turn off check-ins and push notifications at any time.
  • To keep the community safe: review reports, remove content that breaks our Terms of Use and suspend accounts.

We do not sell your personal information, we do not show ads and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Legal bases

If you are in the EU/EEA or the UK, we use your data on these legal bases:

  • To provide the service you signed up for (contract): your account, your purchases, offers and deliveries, and Community.
  • Your consent: else AI (section 4) and push notifications. You can withdraw it at any time in Settings or on your iPhone; this does not affect what was done before.
  • Our legitimate interests: picking pieces for your style and size, sending you messages from else about pieces and people you follow, and keeping the community safe. You can object to them at any time (section 7).
  • A legal obligation: keeping purchase records.

"Picked for you" and your style are worked out automatically from your swipes, sizes and loved pieces. This only changes the order and selection of pieces you see: it has no legal or similarly significant effect on you.

4. else AI and Anthropic

else uses AI from Anthropic, our AI provider, for Ask else, to work out your style from your swipes, and to suggest the details of a piece from its photos when you sell. Nothing is sent to Anthropic until you tap Allow on the "Meet else AI" screen, and you can turn it off at any time in Settings, else AI.

When else AI is on, we send Anthropic only what each feature needs: your messages to else and the conversation so far, your swipes, sizes, style and loved pieces, the address you give in the chat, and the photos you choose for suggestions. Anthropic processes this data on our behalf only to provide these features and does not use it to train its models.

If you don't allow else AI, Ask else and the photo suggestions are off and your style is worked out from your swipes without AI. Styling tips and price suggestions for pieces use only information about the piece, never about you.

5. Who we share your data with

Other members see your username, name, profile photo, city, the pieces you sell and what you post in Community. Your email, address, phone, birthday and conversation with else are never shown to other members.

We use these service providers, who process data on our behalf:

  • Amazon Web Services: hosts our servers, database and photos (United States).
  • Anthropic: else AI, only if you allow it (see section 4).
  • Stripe: processes payments.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, Apple Pay, push notifications and weather.
  • Google: Sign in with Google and our email.

We may also disclose information if required by law.

Transfers outside the EU

Our servers and most of our providers are in the United States, so if you live in the EU/EEA or the UK your data is transferred there. We only do so with the safeguards the GDPR requires: the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework for providers certified under it, or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. You can ask us for a copy of these safeguards.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your data while you have an account. When you delete your account (Settings, Delete account), we permanently erase your personal data: your profile, conversation with else, addresses, sizes, swipes, loved pieces, posts and replies. We keep anonymous records of your orders, because the law requires us to keep purchase records. Copies in our backups are erased automatically within 35 days.

7. Your rights

You can see and change your information in the app, clear your conversation with else, turn else AI off, and delete your account at any time. You can also write to else@elsecompany.com to:

  • access your data and get a copy of it;
  • correct it if it is wrong;
  • delete it;
  • restrict how we use it while a request is being resolved;
  • receive it in a portable format (portability), to take it to another service;
  • object to how we use it on the basis of our legitimate interests;
  • withdraw your consent at any time.

We will reply within one month.

If you are in the EU/EEA or the UK, you can also file a complaint with a data protection authority, such as the Spanish Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (aepd.es). If you live in California or another U.S. state with a privacy law, you have the rights that law gives you; we do not sell or share personal information as defined by those laws.

8. Age

else is only for people who are 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone younger. If you believe someone under 18 has joined, contact us and we will delete their account.

9. Security

Your data travels encrypted, passwords are never stored in plain text and access is limited to the people who need it. No method is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your data.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above. If the changes are significant, we will tell you in the app.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Write to else@elsecompany.com.
