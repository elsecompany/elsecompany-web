who we are

ELSE is a new approach to fashion, combining secondhand shopping with artificial intelligence to create a more personal and intuitive experience. We believe that great fashion doesn't always have to be new, and that technology can help us discover the value of what already exists.

what drives us

Our work is guided by the belief that fashion and technology can create a better shopping experience. We value innovation because we see it as a way to challenge traditional shopping habits and explore new possibilities. We believe in personalisation, understanding that everyone's style, needs and preferences are different. We also value conscious consumption, encouraging people to discover and give new life to existing garments instead of always looking for something new. Above all, we want to make technology useful, accessible and human, creating an experience that genuinely makes people's lives easier.

our nature

Artificial intelligence is one of the foundations of ELSE, shaping the way the platform is developed and how users experience fashion. Rather than simply adding AI as an extra feature, we've built the experience around its possibilities, using technology to make secondhand shopping more intuitive, accessible and personal.

At the centre of this experience is your own personal AI assistant, designed to understand your preferences, adapt to your needs and help you throughout your journey. You can interact with it naturally, ask questions, search for specific products, discover new styles, get recommendations or find inspiration. Instead of spending time browsing through endless options, you can simply explain what you're looking for and let your assistant guide you towards relevant discoveries.

our brands

We select the perfect brands for our users, taking care of quality and style, always with the contemporary touch that defines our brand. In our selection, you can find your style without sacrificing premium quality.

discover

Discover a new way to explore fashion. From unique pieces to brands you love, ELSE helps you find items that match your taste, interests and personal style.

connect

AI to find your style

Your personal AI assistant learns from your preferences, understands what you are looking for and helps you navigate a world of fashion that feels more personal. Less searching, more meaningful discoveries.

rediscover

Give fashion another life

Every pre-loved piece has a story worth continuing. ELSE connects great fashion with new owners, giving garments another opportunity to be worn, loved and appreciated.

future

Our vision for the future is to make secondhand fashion the first choice for a new generation of consumers. We want to continue developing ELSE by exploring new possibilities with artificial intelligence, creating an increasingly personalised experience that understands each user's style, preferences and needs.

We aim to expand the possibilities of our personal AI assistant, making it a tool that goes beyond shopping and helps users with everything related to fashion, from discovering new brands and styles to finding inspiration and making more conscious choices. Our goal is to build a smarter, more connected fashion experience that makes secondhand shopping easier and more accessible for everyone.
